# 11 - Can personal QR be used for public Client payments?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Verify against current first-party Malaysian sources whether an MUA may use a personal DuitNow QR or person-to-person DuitNow identity for ongoing commercial Client payments displayed on a public MUASuites booking page. Separate common practice from provider permission and legal/regulatory requirements. Cover PayNet's distinction between personal and business QR/identities, bank or acquirer terms, payer-visible identity, transaction records, fees/limits, disputes/refunds, and whether a sole-proprietor business account or merchant onboarding is required.

The result must say whether the commercialization map can safely override its current beta-only personal-QR rule, what exact conditions would be required, and which points still require Malaysian legal or provider confirmation.

## Answer

Findings: `.scratch/commercialization-launch/research/personal-qr-public-use.md`

Resolution: do not override the beta-only personal-QR rule. Personal QR and P2P DuitNow IDs are technically capable of receiving Client money, but current first-party sources do not establish general provider permission for ongoing commercial payments on a public MUASuites page. Paid launch requires an SSM-linked business route and provider-approved business QR and/or BRN-linked business ID, with payer-visible identity, records, fees/limits, dispute/refund handling and onboarding confirmed. Personal QR may be considered only for a private, time-boxed test after the named provider confirms the exact use case in writing; it is not a public-launch default.
