# 04 - What legal relationship and documents are needed?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01 - What Malaysian obligations apply to the commercialization launch?

## Question

Define the commercial relationship and document set for the launch. The intended model is MUASuites as a SaaS provider to the MUA, with the MUA retaining responsibility for makeup services, client communications, booking terms, cancellations, refunds, and the accuracy of payment approval. Decide which documents or notices are required for the MUA, the public booking client, Founder beta participants, privacy/data handling, acceptable use, liability limits, and public marketing consent, and which items require Malaysian lawyer review before paid launch.

## Answer

MUASuites is the SaaS provider and the MUA is its customer. The MUA contracts with and serves the Client. The MUA owns the makeup service, package promises, client communications, booking acceptance, payment verification, cancellations, rescheduling, refunds, service disputes, and the first response to Client requests about booking data. MUASuites is not the makeup-service provider, marketplace, payment processor, payment holder, or guarantor. MUASuites owns its software, MUA account, SaaS billing, support, security, and misconduct responsibilities, and assists the MUA with platform data requests.

The launch bundle for Malaysian lawyer review is:

- MUA SaaS terms covering PRO fees, manual renewal, suspension, Founder status, revocation, support, acceptable use, IP, confidentiality, data roles, liability, termination, and governing law.
- MUASuites privacy notice for MUA account, SaaS billing, support, security, marketing, vendors, retention, and data-subject contact.
- Client-facing booking, payment, and platform-role notice identifying the MUA as service provider and payment recipient, and MUASuites as the software platform.
- Data-processing schedule covering permitted Client-data use, instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, deletion/return, and incident cooperation.
- Founder Beta terms covering participation, feedback, privacy, acceptable use, support, revocation, and termination.
- Separate written publicity consent for names, logos, testimonials, screenshots, or booking metrics.
- An MUA-owned Client service-terms template or checklist that each MUA completes for cancellation, rescheduling, refund, and service policies.

Carry the split data-role model into review: the MUA is the likely controller for Client service data; MUASuites is the likely processor for that data and an independent controller for its own MUA account, SaaS billing, support, security, and marketing data; vendors may be subprocessors or separate controllers depending on their service. Counsel must confirm the classification, liability caps and carve-outs, electronic contracting, cross-border processing, DPO/registration duties, and any client-facing obligations.
