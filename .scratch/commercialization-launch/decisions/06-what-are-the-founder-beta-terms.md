# 06 - What are the Founder beta terms?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Turn the existing Founder Plan intent into a beta participation decision for up to 10 selected MUAs. Set selection criteria beyond fame, invitation and acceptance mechanics, free-for-life and PRO-equivalent scope, support expectations, feedback obligations, acceptable use, abuse or abandonment handling, revocation rules, privacy/data-use permissions, testimonial and marketing consent, and what happens if a participant leaves the beta or the product changes.

## Answer

Invite up to 10 MUAs selected for reputation/reach, active workflows, product fit, and willingness to test and give feedback. The Founder Beta ends when paid public access opens. A selected MUA receives the manually recorded Founder Plan only after written or click-through acceptance of the beta terms and completion of onboarding.

The Founder Plan remains free for life with current PRO parity and no retroactive fees. It includes the PRO capabilities MUASuites offers while the product operates; if a PRO capability is materially retired, MUASuites should provide an equivalent replacement where practical. It does not guarantee every future feature, uptime, or continued operation of the product.

Founder MUAs owe lightweight real-use feedback: use the product where appropriate, report material issues, and provide periodic feedback. There is no booking quota, guaranteed promotion, or mandatory response-time commitment. Founders receive priority best-effort support through a dedicated WhatsApp channel, with no SLA, uptime guarantee, or feature-roadmap promise.

Service and usage data may be used under the privacy terms to operate, secure, support, and improve MUASuites. Names, logos, testimonials, screenshots, booking metrics, or other identifiable outputs require separate written publicity consent and are never implied by beta acceptance.

For all Founder grants issued in this commercialization effort, revocation is limited to documented serious terms or acceptable-use breach, fraud, abuse, unlawful use, deliberate harm, or a case-by-case finding of abandonment after contact attempts. There is no automatic inactivity timer; low usage, ordinary product criticism, or declining a marketing request is not abandonment. A returning MUA may request reactivation unless the grant was formally revoked or the account was deleted. This commercialization decision supersedes the earlier broad “founder discretion” wording for these launch grants.
