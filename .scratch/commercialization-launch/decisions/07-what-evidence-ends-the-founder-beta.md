# 07 - What evidence ends the Founder beta?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 06 - What are the Founder beta terms?

## Question

Define the measurable graduation gates from the 10-MUA Founder beta to paid public launch. Decide the observation period, activation and repeat-use measures, booking volume or reliability threshold, support burden, payment-flow correctness, willingness-to-pay evidence from non-Founders, legal/document readiness, and who can make the go/no-go call. Include a fallback if the evidence is mixed: extend, narrow, pause, or launch.

## Answer

Run the Founder Beta for at least 12 weeks. The Founder may review earlier, but paid public launch is not eligible until the minimum period and every hard gate are satisfied.

The usage gates are cohort counts: at least 8 of 10 Founders complete onboarding, at least 6 of 10 use a live booking link, and at least 5 of 10 use the product for repeat bookings across separate weeks. The paid-offer gate is at least 3 explicit commitments from non-Founder MUAs to start the published RM 29 monthly or RM 290 yearly plan when launch opens; interviews or compliments alone do not count.

Use a privacy-safe manual scorecard covering cohort status, onboarding, live links, bookings, repeat use, payment-confirmation issues, support issues, incidents, feedback, and paid intent. Do not add behavior tracking before the relevant privacy and legal review. The quality gate is no unresolved critical failure involving data exposure or loss, conflicting bookings, incorrect payment confirmation, slot availability, or completion of a core booking workflow. Support must be sustainable for one operator, with no recurring critical support pattern, unresolved high-severity issue, or bespoke service burden.

Entity, professional legal/accounting review, required notices and terms, privacy/security controls, dedicated banking/QR, and the correct MUA Payment Confirmation workflow are non-negotiable launch gates even if demand is strong. The Founder makes the go/no-go decision. Mixed evidence allows one corrective extension of up to 4 weeks with a written experiment and refreshed scorecard; after that, the Founder must launch, narrow the offer, pause, or end the beta. There is no automatic paid launch.
