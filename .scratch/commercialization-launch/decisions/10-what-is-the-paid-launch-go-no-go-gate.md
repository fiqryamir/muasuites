## Answer

The commercialization route has two launch phases and one hard paid-launch gate.

### Phase 0 - pre-beta readiness

The MUASuites owner/operator must register the SSM sole proprietorship, prepare the Founding MUA terms, privacy/platform notices, data-processing and incident/request processes, fix every P0 item in the [Founder beta launch baseline audit](../audits/founder-beta-launch-baseline.md), verify the production deployment, and create the manual beta scorecard and support/incident log. Each MUA's invite-only personal-QR use requires confirmation from that MUA's provider; public payment surfaces require a provider-approved business QR or BRN-linked identity.

Phase 0 is complete only when real-client data can be handled without the known public-data, receipt-security, payment-confirmation, or corrected-proof failures. Paid banking, tax, and professional signoff are not required to begin a free controlled beta, but the privacy and participation materials are required.

### Phase 1 - Founding MUA beta

Invite up to 10 Founding MUAs under written Founder Plan acceptance. Run for at least 12 weeks and use the privacy-safe manual scorecard. The cohort must reach 8/10 onboarding, 6/10 live booking-link use, 5/10 repeat use across separate weeks, and 3 explicit non-Founder Paid-Intent Commitments for the RM29 monthly or RM290 yearly offer. There must be no unresolved critical failure, support must remain sustainable for one operator, and all payment confirmation and incident workflows must be exercised.

### Phase 2 - paid public launch

Open PRO only after all of the following pass: the beta evidence gates; no critical product/security/payment failure; sustainable support; SSM, tax and invoice process; Malaysian lawyer review of the legal bundle and data/payment boundaries; accountant review of tax treatment; GXBank business-account and static-QR confirmation or CIMB fallback; privacy, receipt-security, vendor and incident controls; corrected pricing/capacity claims; and a clean production deployment. The published offer is RM29 monthly or RM290 yearly with manual renewal. Non-critical improvements may follow launch; hard gates may not.

The MUASuites owner/operator makes the final commercial go/no-go decision. The lawyer signs legal and data classification; the accountant signs tax and invoice treatment; the implementation owner signs product, security, and deployment readiness; each MUA accepts their own service, provider, payment, and refund responsibilities. Mixed beta evidence permits one corrective extension of up to four weeks with a written experiment, after which the owner must launch, narrow, pause, or end the beta. There is no automatic launch.

Handoff artifacts are the commercialization map, all resolved decision tickets, the Malaysian obligation and banking research, the personal-QR research, the Founder beta launch audit, the manual scorecard, the professional-review question list, and the ordered Phase 0-2 checklist above.
