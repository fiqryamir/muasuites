# 03 - Which entity and liability posture governs the paid launch?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01 - What Malaysian obligations apply to the commercialization launch?

## Question

Choose the business and liability posture for MUASuites before paid public launch. Compare the practical sole-proprietorship route with incorporating a Sdn Bhd in light of the product's data responsibilities, MUA SaaS relationship, direct-to-MUA client payments, founder-only operation, expected beta size, liability tolerance, future hiring or investment, and professional advice. Decide the launch gate: whether the beta can run before formalization, what must exist before any paid customer, and what would trigger later incorporation.

## Answer

Use an SSM sole proprietorship for the initial MUASuites route. Register it before inviting a Founder MUA to use real client data. Before real-client beta use, the privacy notice, security controls, and Founder terms must be ready. Before paid public launch, require the dedicated business account and provider-issued QR, Malaysian lawyer and accountant review, and the agreed tax/invoicing process.

Do not set an arbitrary paid-MUA or revenue number for incorporation. Reassess moving to a Sdn Bhd when liability exposure becomes material, a cofounder or investor joins, employees are added, contracts require a company, revenue becomes material, or professional advice says the liability shield is justified. The sole proprietorship route is a launch baseline, not a conclusion that personal liability or insurance exposure is acceptable without review.
