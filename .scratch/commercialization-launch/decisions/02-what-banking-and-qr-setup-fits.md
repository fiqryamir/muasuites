## Question

Compare the practical dedicated-business-banking and DuitNow QR setup available to a Malaysia-first solo SaaS operator on the likely sole-proprietorship route. Use current first-party bank and PayNet/provider sources. Cover eligibility, required documents, opening method, fees or minimum balances, settlement-name behavior, QR issuance, transaction records, and the point at which a personal QR is no longer an acceptable launch fallback.

End with a short comparison that a founder can take to professional review and a list of unresolved preference decisions; do not recommend a bank solely from secondary reviews.

## Answer

Findings: `.scratch/commercialization-launch/research/business-banking-and-qr.md`

Resolution: use an SSM-linked dedicated business account and provider-issued business DuitNow QR as the paid-public-launch baseline. Shortlist GXBank Biz Account for low-friction setup and CIMB Business Current Account plus Basic Package for more explicit merchant-QR and settlement terms. A personal QR is limited to a time-boxed private beta test and is not acceptable in any public client-payment flow or at paid launch.
