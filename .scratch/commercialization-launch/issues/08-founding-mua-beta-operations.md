# 08: Founding MUA beta operations

**What to build:** Operate the controlled Founding MUA Beta for up to 10 participants, including written acceptance, support, scorecard collection, consent handling, and the 12-week graduation review.

**Blocked by:** 04: Commercial copy and notification alignment; 05: Legal, privacy, and Founding MUA acceptance pack; 06: SSM, tax, and banking setup; 07: MUASuites SaaS-renewal flow

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Up to 10 Founding MUAs are selected using fit, reach, active workflows, and willingness to test and provide feedback.
- [ ] Each participant accepts the Founder Plan terms in writing before the grant is recorded.
- [ ] Invite-only personal QR exceptions have provider confirmation and are never used as public profile defaults.
- [ ] Dedicated WhatsApp support, feedback collection, support logging, incident logging, and publicity consent are operational.
- [ ] The manual scorecard records onboarding, live links, bookings, repeat use, payment issues, support, incidents, feedback, and Paid-Intent Commitments.
- [ ] At 12 weeks, the owner/operator can determine whether 8/10 onboarding, 6/10 live-link use, 5/10 repeat use, 3 non-Founding MUA paid commitments, sustainable support, and reliability gates passed.
- [ ] Mixed evidence produces one written four-week corrective extension or a documented narrow/pause/end decision.
