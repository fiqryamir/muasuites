# 10: Paid public launch rollout

**What to build:** Execute the controlled opening of the RM29 monthly and RM290 yearly PRO offer after the beta and production evidence pass. The rollout must be reversible, auditable, and owned by the MUASuites owner/operator.

**Blocked by:** 08: Founding MUA beta operations; 09: Production readiness verification

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The owner/operator records a final go decision with the legal, accounting, banking, product/security, and beta evidence attached.
- [ ] The paid offer, capacity limits, payment boundary, renewal instructions, and support expectations are published consistently.
- [ ] PRO renewal collection uses the selected business account, static business QR, owner approval, and renewal ledger.
- [ ] Public payment surfaces use provider-approved business identity; invite-only personal QR exceptions remain MUA-specific and provider-confirmed.
- [ ] Monitoring begins for renewals, payment mismatches, incidents, support burden, and new critical failures.
- [ ] A rollback, pause, narrow-launch, or correction path is documented before opening paid access.
- [ ] Non-critical improvements are tracked separately from hard launch blockers.
