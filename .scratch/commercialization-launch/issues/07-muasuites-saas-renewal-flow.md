# 07: MUASuites SaaS-renewal flow

**What to build:** Give a PRO MUA a complete manual renewal path from choosing a period to owner verification and plan extension, using MUASuites' separate business QR and renewal ledger.

**Blocked by:** 02: Proof-of-Transfer confidentiality; 06: SSM, tax, and banking setup

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An MUA can choose RM29 monthly or RM290 yearly at each renewal.
- [ ] The renewal page displays only MUASuites' provider-issued business QR and clear transfer instructions.
- [ ] Uploading renewal proof creates a pending renewal and never activates PRO automatically.
- [ ] The owner/operator can review proof, bank reference, period, amount, and MUA, then approve or reject manually.
- [ ] Approval extends the correct expiry without losing remaining paid time and records the renewal ledger entry.
- [ ] Mismatched, duplicate, overpaid, and mistaken transfers remain held for manual reconciliation.
- [ ] MUA plan status, reminders, ledger history, and renewal outcome are consistent.
