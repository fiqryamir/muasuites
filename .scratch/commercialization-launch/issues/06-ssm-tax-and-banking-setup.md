# 06: SSM, tax, and banking setup

**What to build:** Execute the business and payment-operations handoff for Malaysia-first paid launch. Use the sole-proprietorship route, GXBank first, and CIMB as fallback only after the required provider confirmations.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The SSM sole proprietorship registration and exact business activity/name are completed or handed to the licensed professional with evidence.
- [ ] TIN and tax/invoice record responsibilities are confirmed with the accountant.
- [ ] GXBank Biz Account eligibility, business use, static QR, payer-visible name, limits, fees, records, settlement, and dispute handling are confirmed.
- [ ] CIMB Basic Package is documented as the fallback if GXBank fails the confirmation checklist.
- [ ] The selected provider-issued business QR or BRN-linked business identity is ready for MUASuites SaaS renewals.
- [ ] The renewal ledger, monthly reconciliation process, record retention, and manual mismatch/refund process are defined.
- [ ] Provider and professional confirmations are stored as launch evidence without committing secrets.
