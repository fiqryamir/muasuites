# 02 — Bot protection posture

**Type:** grilling (HITL)
**Status:** resolved

## Question

How aggressive should bot protection be, given that Clients open Booking Links from Instagram's in-app browser and MUAs want their public profiles findable on Google?

## Answer

**Moderate.** Preserve Google/Bing crawling and Instagram in-app WebView click-throughs; block known AI/spam crawlers and scanner junk cheaply; no blanket blocking and no Under-Attack mode. The human explicitly rejected the aggressive and maximum postures out of conversion-path and SEO risk.
