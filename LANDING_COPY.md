## S4 — How it works

**Section title:**
> How a booking actually happens

**Step 1 — A client checks you out**
> She opens your studio page, sees your live availability and a travel estimate for her venue — then WhatsApps you. The message is already half-written for her.

**Step 2 — You reply with a link**
> You confirm the date, then generate a booking link in under a minute: travel fee auto-calculated from your rate, any surcharge with its reason attached, your price, your deposit. Sent in the same chat you were already in.

**Step 3 — She books like a checkout**
> Five calm steps on her phone: date, package, event details, deposit by DuitNow QR, receipt uploaded. Her slot is held for ten minutes while she pays — no one else can take it.

**Step 4 — You approve, and it's booked**
> Telegram pings you with the receipt attached. You check the transfer in your own banking app, tap approve — the date is blocked and lands in your calendar automatically.

## S6 — Features

**Section title:**
> Everything after the chat, handled

**1. Live availability calendar**
> Clients check your open dates themselves, before they ever message you. You stop answering the same question thirty times a month.

**2. Travel fees without the maths**
> Set your rate once. The fee from your studio to their venue is calculated automatically — and you can override it for any booking.

**3. Deposits your way**
> A fixed amount or a percentage, configured once in your settings — adjusted per booking whenever you need to.

**4. Telegram, your front desk**
> New bookings arrive with the receipt attached. Approve or decline right from the chat — no dashboard required.

**5. Surcharges with a reason**
> Early call time? Out-of-state venue? Add the fee and its reason. Clients see it itemised on the quote — considered, not invented.

**6. Double-booking-proof**
> A paid slot locks the date for everyone else. Two brides, one morning — never again.

## S10 — FAQ

**Section title:**
> Fair questions

**Do my clients need to download an app?**
> No. Everything runs from the link you send — it opens in their browser and disappears when they're done.

**Do I need to switch calendar apps?**
> No. Confirmed bookings arrive as a standard calendar file that opens in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and whatever else you already use.

**What if someone uploads a fake receipt?**
> Nothing confirms automatically. You check the transfer in your own banking app first — a screenshot alone never books a date.

**What happens when I hit my free-plan limit?**
> Existing bookings continue as normal. New link generation pauses until a slot frees up or you upgrade — we'll never charge you without asking.

**Can I still take cash or direct bookings?**
> Yes. Generate the link yourself and approve it directly — the date is still blocked and in your calendar.

**What if a client enters the wrong details?**
> Decline the booking with a reason, or send a corrected link. You're never locked into a booking you didn't approve.
